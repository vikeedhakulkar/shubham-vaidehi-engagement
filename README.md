# Shubham & Vaidehi — Engagement Invitation 💍

A luxury digital engagement invitation website for **Shubham & Vaidehi**.

- **Couple**: Shubham & Vaidehi
- **Date**: 14 February 2028 (Monday)
- **Venue**: Nagpur, Maharashtra, India
- **Live Website**: [https://vikeedhakulkar.github.io/shubham-vaidehi-engagement/](https://vikeedhakulkar.github.io/shubham-vaidehi-engagement/)

---

## ✨ Features

- **Interactive Wax Seal Envelope**: Click-to-open interactive engagement letter animation with monogram seal `S&V`.
- **Live Countdown Timer**: Real-time countdown clock counting down to 14 February 2028.
- **Background Music**: Built-in background audio player with play/pause controls.
- **Event & Celebration Details**: Date, location, and ceremony highlights.
- **Add to Calendar & WhatsApp Share**: Direct calendar integration and WhatsApp share link with pre-filled message.
- **Responsive & Elegant Design**: Gold & ivory aesthetic with botanical illustrations and typography.
